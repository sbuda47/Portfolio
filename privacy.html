Privacy notice

This portfolio belongs to Sibusiso Mpofu. You can browse my work without an account. If you contact me, here is how your information is handled.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

01

When you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address, and message so I can respond to your enquiry and discuss potential work. I do not use these messages for marketing.

Formspree processes form submissions and delivers them to my inbox. It may also process technical information such as your IP address and browser details for delivery, security, and spam prevention. Its processing may take place outside your country, including in the United States. Read Formspree's privacy policy for its practices.

Submitting the form takes you to Formspree, which may ask you to complete a CAPTCHA security check before accepting your message.

Please do not include passwords, identity documents, confidential client information, or other sensitive data. You can also use the direct email link on the Contact page instead of the form.

02

Browsing and external links

The site does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of its own. Fonts, images, and project videos are hosted with the site. There are no embedded social-media feeds.

GitHub Pages hosts this portfolio and may log technical information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation. See GitHub's privacy statement. Following links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or another external service takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

03

Keeping and managing messages

Correspondence may be held in my inbox and Formspree account for as long as needed to respond and maintain a relevant record of our discussion. Service providers may retain their own security records under their policies.

To ask about your information, or request access, correction, or deletion of a message, email s.mpofu.m.01@gmail.com. Include enough detail to identify the enquiry, but do not send sensitive documents. I will review your request and any records I control.
