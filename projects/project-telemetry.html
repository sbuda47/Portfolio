What I built

Real air-quality sensor data is not clean — sensors drop out, drift, and fail silently. I built the quality-control and conditioning stage that the team’s modulation and monitoring subsystems depended on, screening every sensor stream against missing-data and validity criteria, interpolating short gaps while preserving long outages as genuine segment boundaries, and comparing two smoothing filters before selecting the Savitzky-Golay filter for its stronger peak retention on pollution spike events.

One sensor with a deceptively low 0.84% missing-data rate was flagged and excluded after quality control revealed 67% of its readings were invalid — a check a simple completeness threshold would have missed.

What the team shipped

  • 5-scheme modulation comparison (AM reached 15.4 dB recovered SNR)
  • Digital telemetry chain with zero-error bit recovery
  • Six-tab Streamlit dashboard for live monitoring and alerts
