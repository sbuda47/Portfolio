CNC Simulator & G-code Visualizer

A lexer and parser for RS-274 G-code that tracks machine state and renders the toolpath before hardware. Catches coordinate typos, sequencing errors, and unsafe modal states at your desk — preventing costly tool crashes and part failures before they happen.

Why I built it

Bad G-code doesn't announce itself — a typo'd coordinate or a wrongly sequenced command can crash the tool or ruin a part before you know something's wrong. I built a lexer and parser for RS-274 G-code from scratch in Python, tracking the machine's modal state across every line and rendering the resulting toolpath for visual validation before hardware.

The tool is built for personal and small-batch use, serving as a first-pass sanity check for G-code validation. It's intentionally scoped for the engineering workflow I use, not as a production safety validator.

What it catches

  • Malformed or mistyped commands, flagged at the exact line
  • Out-of-sequence or invalid modal state
  • Visual mismatch between intended and actual toolpath
